# Buddy's Money Day

A 5-minute, fully-voiced phone game that teaches a 4-year-old the first principles of money: effort earns coins, saving is for a goal you chose, needs come before wants, and sharing is its own reward. No reading required.

## Language

**Coin**:
The game's unit of value. Earned only through Helping Tasks; spent only at the Shelf, the Friend beat, or Buddy's food bowl.
_Avoid_: money, points, credits, cash

**Helping Task**:
A ~10-second drag or tap activity that ends with a payment of Coins (tidy the toys, water the tree). Two run per Money Day.
_Avoid_: chore, minigame, job, quest

**Money Day**:
One play session: greet Buddy → Helping Tasks → hunger → Friend → Shelf → jars ritual → tuck-in. A Goal lands after 2–3 Money Days.
_Avoid_: level, round, session, run

**Goal**:
The item the child chose to save for, shown as a picture. Costs 6 Coins. A finished Goal becomes a real object in Home.
_Avoid_: prize, reward, target, wish

**Save Jar**:
The visible jar holding Coins toward the current Goal, and the default destination for every Coin not deliberately spent.
_Avoid_: piggy bank, savings account, bank

**Temptation**:
The shiny take-it-now item at the Shelf (the lollipop). Buying it is always allowed and always praised; the Goal simply takes one more Money Day.
_Avoid_: trap, bad buy, impulse, mistake

**Friend**:
A character with a problem a Coin can fix (the bird's broken swing). Giving is optional at every amount, including zero, with no penalty.
_Avoid_: charity, donation, NPC, stranger

**Buddy**:
The child's plush creature: gets hungry every Money Day (the Need) and lives in Home. Never punished, never permanently sad.
_Avoid_: pet, avatar, mascot, character

**Hunger**:
Buddy's daily need beat. Feeding costs 1 Coin; skipping leaves Buddy droopy until fed on any later day — the consequence is visible and always fixable.
_Avoid_: required purchase, health, energy bar

**Home**:
Buddy's room. Fills with the real objects the child saved for; the Home strip is the entire progression system (no levels, no XP).
_Avoid_: inventory, collection menu, shop

**Grown-up Setup**:
The only text screen: first-run name entry and game reset. Exists for the parent, never required for play.
_Avoid_: parent mode, settings, menu, dashboard
