# Buddy's Money Day

A 5-minute phone game that teaches a 4-year-old the first principles of money: work earns coins, saving is for the dream you chose, buying is a choice, and caring for Buddy is never a cost. The child plays by pictures — no reading required; every line is mirrored on screen for a grown-up to read along, and voice is optional.

## Language

**Coin**:
The game's unit of value. Earned only through Helping Tasks; spent only at the Store. Saving is the default — every Coin not deliberately spent goes to the jar.
_Avoid_: money, points, credits, cash

**Helping Task**:
A ~10-second drag or tap activity that ends with a payment of Coins (tidy the toys, water the tree, feed the bear). Three run per Money Day — Tidy pays 2, Water 1, Feed 1 — each once, in any order. Feeding is paid like the rest: care is rewarded, never a cost.
_Avoid_: minigame, quest

**Money Day**:
One play session: greet Buddy → the three Helping Tasks (any order) → the Store (buy a toy or save) → tuck-in. A Dream toy lands after 2–3 Money Days.
_Avoid_: level, round, session, run

**Dream toy**:
The big toy the child chose to save for — the wagon, then the big teddy — shown as a picture. Costs 12 Coins. A finished Dream toy becomes a real object in My Toys; the pedestal then offers the next dream, and once every dream is owned the list cycles, so the loop never dead-ends.
_Avoid_: prize, reward, target, wish, goal

**Save Jar**:
The visible jar holding Coins toward the current Dream toy — the jar is the dream's — and the default destination for every Coin not deliberately spent.
_Avoid_: piggy bank, savings account, bank

**Store**:
The day's decision beat: a shelf of priced toys (slice 1: the ball alone) beside the dream pedestal. The big button saves the rest; buying is a deliberate second action. An unaffordable toy gets an honest compare, never a reproach.
_Avoid_: shop, marketplace, storefront

**Buddy**:
The child's plush creature and the day's host. Feeding is one of the three paid Helping Tasks — no cost and no skip. Never punished, never permanently sad.
_Avoid_: pet, avatar, mascot, character

**My Toys**:
The room behind the title door where owned toys live on a rug; tapping a toy opens its mini-game (slice 1: the ball's keepy-uppy). Empty on a fresh install — a gentle hint, never a scolding. Progression is what's on the rug: no levels, no XP.
_Avoid_: Home, inventory, collection menu

**Plan**:
The morning board on the greeting screen: today's three Helping Tasks as picture cards with their coin prices (Tidy 2 · Water 1 · Feed 1), checkmarks as each finishes, next to the dream's progress. The day's whole deal in one look.
_Avoid_: schedule, agenda, task list

**Dream Banner**:
The always-visible strip showing the current Dream toy's picture and its twelve coin slots, filled as Coins are saved. It previews outcomes at the Store and lands the toy in My Toys at dream-reached.
_Avoid_: progress bar, counter, HUD widget

**Recap**:
The tuck-in line that closes every Money Day: what was earned today and where the dream stands — honest in every variant, never a scolding.
_Avoid_: summary, report card, stats

**Grown-up Setup**:
The only text screen: first-run name entry, the Voice switch and Buddy's actor choice (voice is off until a grown-up turns it on), and game reset. Exists for the parent, never required for play.
_Avoid_: parent mode, settings, menu, dashboard
